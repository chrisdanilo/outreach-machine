# Reply Templates for Declines

These are built from Chris's own replies, not invented. Every line below appears in at least one reply he actually sent. `{Name}`, `{Weekday}` and `{Org}` are the only fields that vary.

## What the data says (25 declines, 20 replies)

| Pattern | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Opener** | "Thanks for this very human reply/response." Used in 16 of the 20 replies |
| **Close** | "I'm easy to find." Used in 12 of 20. He never asks for a meeting and never argues with the no |
| **Something free** | Included in 14 of 20: the YouTube training-design tutorial 11×, the Starter Kit 3× |
| **Length** | Most are 3–8 lines |
| **"I'll stop following up"** | Used only on keep-on-file replies |
| **Sign-off** | `Happy {Weekday}!` or `Be well,` → `c` → signature |
| **Response rate after his reply** | 0 of 19 cold decliners wrote back. These replies are for goodwill, not for conversion |
| **Removal requests** | 2 so far (Nassau, CHCF). Chris has never replied to one, so there is no precedent. Template R is new and marked as such |

## Which template to use

| They said… | Category | Template | Asset? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Not interested" / "we do it in-house" / "we already use X" | Hard no | **A** | Optional: tutorial |
| "Not at this time" / "our calendar is full" / "not this year" | Not now | **B** | Tutorial |
| "We'll keep you on file" / "we'll reach out if needed" / "when we have RFPs" | Keep on file | **C** | Starter Kit |
| "I've passed this to the team, they'll contact you if it's a fit" | Forwarded, no commitment | **D** | Tutorial |
| "Remove me" / "take me off your list" / "unsubscribe" | Removal request | **R** | **None** |

## R · Removal request (🆕 no precedent, Chris to approve)

>
> Done. You won't hear from me again. Thanks for letting me know, and thanks for the important work you're doing.

**Rules for R:** send nothing else. No asset, no "easy to find," and no weekday greeting. Remove only the person who asked unless they said "each email" or "everyone" (Nassau did). Tag them `delete_declined` in HubSpot.
**Alternative:** don't reply at all. Pros: zero further contact, which is what they asked for. Cons: they never learn the request was honored, and a confirmation is the courteous norm.
