**Subject:** `Question about your conference at {Short} . . .`

{Names},

My name is Chris Danilo and we haven't met yet.

I'm reaching out to find **the right person to talk to about conference design.**

For the last few years, I've been training educators across the country on "**Human Skills**."

What I've found is:

Most conferences are <u>**all about the speaker**</u> or <u>**all about the information**</u>.

So, I've been designing keynotes, trainings, and conferences to get these results:

- Instant audience c**onnection**
- **Highly engaged** attention
- Learning that converts to **action**

**https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iNCItCda-8g**

I've been having good results with **ChildCare Aware** chapters and **CCR&R**s across the US, so I'm reaching out to groups like yours who have similar audiences and goals.

**Is it worth a conversation to see if there's a fit?**

Be well,

c

P.S. I attached an easy-to-share and review Speaker Guide, too.
